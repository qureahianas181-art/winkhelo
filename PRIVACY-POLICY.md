# Privacy Policy — WinKhelo

WinKhelo is a free browser-based game demo. It does not ask for a name, email, phone number, payment details, or location.

The game stores a small XP score locally in your browser using localStorage. This data stays on the device/browser unless the user clears browser data. The site has no account system, analytics, advertising, payments, or cash prizes in this demo.

Contact: Add your own contact email before public promotion.
